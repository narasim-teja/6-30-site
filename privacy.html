Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

6:30 collects no data. Nothing you type, no alarm, and no usage information ever leaves your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Alarms. Alarms you set are scheduled with Apple's alarm system (AlarmKit) and stored by iOS on your device. 6:30 has no server and doesn't copy them anywhere.
  • One preference. 6:30 remembers whether you've set an alarm before, only to stop showing the "Type a time" hint. It's stored on your device.
  • Calculations. What you type is used to calculate or set an alarm, and isn't stored.

What 6:30 doesn't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or personal information.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No network requests. The app works fully offline.

Permissions

6:30 asks for permission to schedule alarms the first time you set one. You can turn it off any time in Settings. 6:30 asks for no other permissions.

Children

6:30 collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
