Help with 6:30 · Calculator Alarm

Set an alarm

Type a time on the keypad and press =. The display confirms it, for example “6:30 AM · Alarm set · rings in 8h 49m”.

  • 630: three digits always mean morning, so this is 6:30 AM.
  • 1830: four digits are 24-hour time, so this is 6:30 PM.
  • 0030: half past midnight.

Anything that isn't a valid time, such as 975 or 12+3×4, calculates normally.

See or delete alarms

Tap the round alarm button at the top left. Each alarm shows when it rings. Swipe left on one to delete it.

Snooze and stop

When an alarm rings, tap Snooze for 9 more minutes. A countdown with a Stop button appears on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

“Alarms are off”

6:30 needs permission to schedule alarms. Tap the message on the display to open Settings, then allow alarms for 6:30.

Does it ring in Silent mode?

Yes. 6:30 uses Apple's alarm system, so alarms ring through Silent mode and Focus, just like Clock alarms. Alarm volume follows your iPhone's alarm volume setting.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26.1 or later.
